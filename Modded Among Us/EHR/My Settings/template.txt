Help:
<size=200%><align=center><b><color=#d50d6c>♡ ENDLESS HOST ROLES ♡</color>
<color=#f89b29>✽ QUICK GUIDE ✽</color></b></align></size>

<b><size=50%><align=center>(Guide by @iam._.steve on Discord)</color></align></size></b>

<size=80%>EHR is a pretty complicated mod, but don't worry! I'm here to teach you all the basics of the game so you can become the best player there is! First off, let's start with the most integral part of the mod...</color></size>

<size=150%><align=center><b><color=#d50d6c>✽ ROLES ✽</color></b></align></size>

<size=80%>Each player will be assigned a role at the start of the game. There are 4 role types: <b><color=#ff1919>Impostor</color>, <color=#8cffff>Crewmate</color>, <color=#222222>Neutral</color>, and Add-Ons</color>.</b></color></size>

<color=#ff1919><b>Impostors & <color=#8cffff>Crewmates</b></color>
Self-explanatory. These roles are given to the <color=#ff1919>Impostors</color> or <color=#8cffff>Crewmates</color>.</color>

<color=#222222><b>Neutrals</b></color>
This isn't a team. These roles are for roles that don't side with the <color=#ff1919>Impostors</color> or <color=#8cffff>Crewmates</color>. Usually, they work alone toward a goal, depending on what their role does. That goal can range from getting their target voted out to win alone, killing everyone in the game, simply staying alive until the end to win with the winning team, and more.</color>

<b>Add-Ons</b></color>
Add-Ons are roles that you can get on top of your team role. Usually, they provide minor benefits, such as your killer automatically self-reporting your body, giving you a red screen flash when a body dies, and more. Some are even major additions, like being able to kill people after your death. But be wary, as you may be assigned a harmful Add-On! These roles may give you reduced vision, an inability to report bodies, an inability to fix sabotages, and more. Remember that most (not all) Add-Ons are not team-specific and can be assigned to anyone unless the host individually chooses which roles go on what.</color>

It is important to remember that nobody should reveal their role unless they have a reason to do so (e.g. <color=#b8fb4f><b>Snitch</b></color> finishing their tasks to out the <color=#ff1919>Impostors</color>).</color>

<size=150%><align=center><b><color=#d50d6c>✽ CHAT COMMANDS ♡</color></b></align></size>

Chat commands are a very useful asset, and each one has its own useful effect. Examples of some meeting commands can be found below:</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>/m:</b></color> Explains your role and Add-On(s) in detail. <b>A MUST USE</b> if you don't know what your role does.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>/r [role name]:</b></color> Shows you what any role in the game does. Just type in the role. Typing /r on its own shows every role enabled in the host's settings.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>/kcount (can be disabled by host):</b></color> Displays the amount of <color=#ff1919>Impostors</color> and <color=#222222>Neutral</color> Killers remaining.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>/l (not i, L):</b></color> Used in the lobby to show the results of the previous game. Displays who died to whom, everyone's roles, and who won the last game. Usually, hosts will make this message appear automatically after the round ends.</color>

<size=150%><align=center><b><color=#d50d6c>✽ GUESSING ✽</color></b></align></size>

Guessing, or variants of guessing, are abilities that can be used by a select few roles. Usually, they involve <b>guessing a certain player's role</b> in order to kill them during a meeting or achieve other effects. Sometimes, hosts will make it so entire teams or even all players can guess.</color>

<size=150%><align=center><b><color=#d50d6c>✽ HOW TO GUESS ✽</color></b></align></size>

This guide will show the most common way to guess using the /bt command.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>#1. Figure out the player's role</b></color>
Before you can guess, you need to find out what their role is. If you've found out what their role is, you can guess them in the next meeting. For example, I know Blue is the <color=#b8fb4f><b>Snitch</b></color>, so moving on to step 2...</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>#2. Type /id</b></color>
Typing /id will show a list of everyone and their respective ID, represented by a number from 1-15. I type /id and Blue's ID is 7. Sometimes, you may already see the IDs of all players without having to type this command. Now all that's left is...</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>#3. Type /bt [player ID] [role]</b></color>
In my case, it would be /bt 7 <color=#b8fb4f>snitch</color>. Now, if correct, I have killed Blue, and now Blue will have to wallow in their shame as a ghost. How sad. But if I misguess, I have to wallow in my shame as I die if I guess wrong. How embarrassing.</color>

Now, you should be caught up on all things EHR-related. Now go out there and win some games!</color>


FAQ:

<size=175%><align=center><b><color=#d50d6c>♡</color> <color=#f89b29>Frequently Asked Questions</color> <color=#d50d6c>♡</color></b></align></size>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. I'm new. Where should I start?</b></color>
Read <b>/t Help</b> first, then <b>/t Commands</b>.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. I'm dead. Should I leave?</b></color>
No. Stay until the game ends. Some roles can still use abilities after death.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. How do I know what my role does?</b></color>
Use <b>/m</b> during the game.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. How do I see all active roles?</b></color>
Use <b>/r</b> during the game.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. How do I check a specific role?</b></color>
Use <b>/r [role_name]</b>.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. When can I use /m and /r?</b></color>
Only during a game. They cannot be used in the lobby.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. How do I see player IDs?</b></color>
Use <b>/id</b>.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. How do I guess someone's role?</b></color>
Find their ID using <b>/id</b>, then use <b>/bt [Player_ID] [Role]</b>.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. What happens if I guess wrong?</b></color>
Depending on the host settings and your role, you may die.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. What is an Add-On?</b></color>
An Add-On gives extra abilities or disadvantages in addition to your main role.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. Can I have both a role and an Add-On?</b></color>
Yes.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. Should I reveal my role?</b></color>
Usually no, unless your role benefits from revealing it.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. Why can't I use some commands?</b></color>
Some commands only work during meetings, while alive, or for specific roles.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. Why can't I use my ability?</b></color>
Your role may have conditions before it can use its ability. Read <b>/m</b>.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. Why can't I report bodies or fix sabotages?</b></color>
Some Add-Ons or roles disable these abilities.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. Why is my vision different?</b></color>
Your role, Add-On, or lobby settings may change your vision.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. Why did I suddenly die?</b></color>
Another role's ability, an Add-On, or a guessing role may have killed you.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. Why didn't the game end?</b></color>
Another team's win condition has not been met yet.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. Why can't I vent?</b></color>
Only certain roles can use vents.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. Can ghosts still do anything?</b></color>
Yes. Some roles and Add-Ons allow ghosts to use abilities or complete tasks.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. What are the four role types?</b></color>
<color=#8cffff>Crewmate</color>, <color=#ff1919>Impostor</color>, <color=#222222>Neutral</color>, and Add-On</color>.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. Where can I see every available command?</b></color>
Use <b>/help</b>.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. Where can I see every available tag?</b></color>
Use <b>/t</b>.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. What should I do if I'm confused?</b></color>
Read <b>/t Help</b>, <b>/t Commands</b>, and <b>/m</b>.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. Can I leave in the middle of a game?</b></color>
Please don't. It ruins the experience for everyone.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. Are beginners welcome?</b></color>
Absolutely! Everyone starts somewhere. Just be willing to learn.</color>

<color=#f89b29><b>Q. I still need help.</b></color>
DM <b>@iam._.steve</b></color> on Discord.</color>
